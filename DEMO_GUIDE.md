# FINN reviewer walkthrough

Estimated time: three minutes. Start with Scenario replay; no key is required. The Groww-inspired interface opens on Holdings. Tap a row for its dark EOD chart, use Sort and the price/change toggle, then select Explore for the daily brief.

| Step | Action | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read the Brief, then expand lower-priority filings | A clear attention hierarchy without losing the filing inventory |
| 2 | Change 24h to 3d or 7d | A real timestamp window; older scenarios appear |
| 3 | Open the result scenario | Revenue/EBITDA/PBT/PAT and deterministic QoQ/YoY arithmetic, explicitly illustrative |
| 4 | Save a filing and mark it read | Persistent browser-local triage |
| 5 | Open Rules; change volume from 2× to 3× | Configurable signal thresholds; price and volume rules work independently |
| 6 | Open the Infosys guidance check | A period-matched, historical promise/outcome comparison, not just a summary |
| 7 | Inspect S1/S2, then open their PDF links | Transparent evidence, page identities and primary documents |
| 8 | Research another bundled company | One reusable research flow across four companies |
| 9 | Research LTFOODS | Unseeded ticker discovery; a report size limit can coexist with available transcript evidence |
| 10 | Switch to Live providers | Real upstream integrations, timestamps, partial coverage and honest failure states |
| 11 | Open your avatar or Profile; view Your activity | Research, filing, chart and preference actions appear with timestamps and data labels |
| 12 | Open Reports & statements, choose a period, preview and download | A working CSV report of local activity, research, saved filings or the current portfolio snapshot |
| 13 | Use the moon/sun header button or Profile → Appearance; reload | Full light/dark themes with a remembered browser preference |

Profile activity and the local display name survive reloads in the same browser. A new browser starts with an honest empty state. Saved filing snapshots remain available after changing the digest window. Profile activity does not sync between devices.

Try on a phone or resize below 640 px: bottom navigation appears, holdings become compact rows with sparklines, research controls wrap, and chart details fit within a modal. The larger layout uses a header and portfolio sidebar. The EOD chart has a five-session range and an all-available-observations range; it does not manufacture intraday candles.

The most important distinction: **scenario prices and announcements are synthetic; company research references actual, dated primary documents**. The app does not claim live result-table extraction, background push delivery or an always-available exchange feed.
